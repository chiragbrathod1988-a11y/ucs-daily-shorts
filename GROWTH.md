# Growth playbook — titles, descriptions, hashtags (modelled on Neeraj Joshi, 5.33M subs)

Analysed 1 Nov 2026 via vidIQ: ~100 of his Shorts and top long videos. He gained +60k subs and +15.6M views in 30 days.
Every Short/Reel posted for UpperCircuitStocks (automatic or hand-edited) follows these rules. Both `post_text.py`
scripts already apply them; use this file for one-off reels (Zoom edits etc.).

1. **Title = search phrase + promise, under 60 characters.** "X in 1 Min", "How to …", "… Explained", "X vs Y: Which One?".
   One emoji at most. No dates or #hashtags in evergreen titles.
2. **Shorts description = 3 hashtags.** The first 3 show above the title on YouTube, and more than that dilutes them. He uses
   broad, high-volume tags: `#trading #tradingforbeginners #stockmarket` + one topic tag.
   → We keep our links (Chirag's rule) but end with exactly 3 hashtags and open with one plain keyword sentence.
3. **Evergreen beginner education beats daily news.** His top Shorts are timeless (patterns, RSI, EMA, stop-loss,
   scams, trader stories) and keep getting views for years. Daily-level videos get attention for about a day.
4. **Numbered daily series.** "Trading Course Day 1 … 100" builds binge-watching and a habit. Each episode is 2–3 min.
5. **Consistent slots:** he posts daily at 8:00 or 10:00 IST.
6. **Long videos:** a full SEO description, made of a 2–3 line keyword summary, link blocks, a "Topic Covered:" list of 20 search phrases,
   a disclaimer, 3 hashtags, plus ~20 YouTube tags (English + Hinglish, e.g. "trading kaise kare in hindi").
7. **Story / shock hooks** for reach: "Fat Finger Trade 😯", "Engineer Robs Bank For Stock Market", "Scam Alert".

## Rules for our posts
- YouTube title ≤ 60 chars, keyword first: `Nifty Prediction 3 Nov 🔥 25,900 Todega ya 25,600?`, `Gold Crash Explained 📉`.
- Description: line 1 = plain keyword sentence. Then the hook body, Telegram, AI Dashboard, the 4 course links (verbatim), subscribe line,
  disclaimer, then **exactly 3 hashtags** (broad + topic).
- Tags: 15–20 search phrases, mixing English and Hinglish ("nifty kal kya karega", "trading for beginners").
- Instagram: the same keyword first line (IG search reads captions) and 5 hashtags.
- Never: profit/₹ claims, signals/targets/SL/exits on YouTube, invented data.
